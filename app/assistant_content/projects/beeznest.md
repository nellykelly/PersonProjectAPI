# What it is

Beeznest was a B2B business social network — think of it as Facebook for companies rather
than people. Businesses could connect with one another to find and build new
partnerships: they could become friends, direct-message each other, and post content on
their own company page. The goal was to give businesses a channel for exposure and
collaboration, and in effect to become a searchable phonebook of companies.

# Build and context

It was built with Ruby on Rails and SQLite. It is one of Nelson's earlier projects,
predating the Flask work on this site, and it is included here mainly for range — it
shows Rails and a different problem domain (social graph, messaging, company profiles)
rather than the trading and infrastructure focus of the flagship projects.

Beeznest took **2nd place at the StreetCode Accelerator Demo Day**, which is the concrete
outside validation attached to it.

The code is on GitHub at https://github.com/nellykelly/BezzNest.

# What it demonstrates

Full-stack web development in a second framework (Rails), modelling a social graph with
friending, direct messaging and per-company content pages, and taking a product far
enough to place in an accelerator demo day.
