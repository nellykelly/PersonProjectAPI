# Who Nelson is

Nelson Koskela is a software engineer based in Houston, Texas. From July 2022 until
September 2026 he was a Software Engineer II at JPMorgan Chase & Co., in the Corporate &
Investment Banking technology organisation; he had joined the year before as a Software
Engineering Program (SEP) intern in 2021. He is now working independently, including on
the projects on this site.

He holds a BS in Computer Science from Cogswell University of Silicon Valley, after
beginning his studies at Howard University.

Before engineering, Nelson trained in classical vocal performance at the Ruth Asawa
School of the Arts in San Francisco. He tends to describe himself as blending a
technical and a creative background rather than choosing between them.

# How he works

Python was a daily part of his work at JPMorgan, not something picked up on the side, and
it is what he keeps building with: the independent projects on this site are in trading
systems, financial data pipelines, CI/CD tooling, and network and traffic diagnostics.

The projects are deliberately built to a production standard rather than as quick demos:
real queues and workers, real databases (Postgres and Redis), containerised with Docker,
tested with a full pytest suite, and deployed to a live server. The point of each one is
to demonstrate a specific competency an engineering interviewer screens for.
