What we do

Your one-stop espresso and coffee suppliers.

We're a full service organization that partners with companies in the food and beverage industry. Working with you, our goal is to keep your cafes and restaurants operating optimally and efficiently.

  • 01

    Whole bean roasting

    Small batch, roasted to order. Private label packaging available for all your espressos and coffees.

  • 02

    Commercial equipment

    New and used espresso machines, espresso and coffee grinders, coffee brewers, panini grills, blenders, granita machines and slicers.

  • 03

    Repair & preventive maintenance

    Troubleshooting, repairs and emergency call outs. Our preventive maintenance service intervals are 45-60 days, based on your volume of use.

  • 04

    Barista training

    Barista training programs for your team.

  • 05

    Cafe design & build-outs

    From concept to reality, we're with you every step. Consultation for current and future coffee business owners.

  • 06

    Products & cafe essentials

    Start up kits, tamps, knock boxes, steaming pitchers, water filtration systems, cleaners, mugs and cups.
