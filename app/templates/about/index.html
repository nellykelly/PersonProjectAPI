About

I'm a Software Engineer II at JPMorgan Chase & Co., Corporate & Investment Banking, in Houston, TX, where I've worked since July 2022 after starting as a Software Engineering Program (SEP) intern in 2021. Python is a tool I use daily there, not something I'm learning on the side -- the projects below are me continuing to build with it outside of work, not picking it up for the first time.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from Cogswell University of Silicon Valley, after starting my undergraduate studies at Howard University. Before that, I trained in classical vocal performance at the Ruth Asawa School of the Arts -- a background that still shapes how I think about craft, precision, and performing under pressure, even in an engineering context.

Outside of work, I build independent projects spanning trading systems, financial data pipelines, CI/CD-style pipeline visualization, and network diagnostics -- you'll find them under Projects. I like combining a technical and creative background: rigorous where it counts, expressive where it helps.
