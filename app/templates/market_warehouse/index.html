Technical & risk indicators

Sharpe/Sortino assume a 0% risk-free rate (no real T-bill data source in this project) -- directionally useful, not precise.

Price projection

Not a forecast. Not financial advice. Three different, real forecasting assumptions, not one model tuned three ways -- Linear trend extrapolates recent momentum; Random walk (drift) is the textbook no-skill baseline every real forecast gets benchmarked against; Mean reversion assumes the opposite of trend-following, that price pulls back toward its own recent average. None has any demonstrated ability to predict real future prices -- stock prices are close to a random walk. Watch the R² figure (blank for Random walk, which has no analogous fit statistic): a low value means the assumption barely fits the recent data at all.

From fct_security_price_projection -- every method x lookback-window combination is precomputed by dbt; the pickers below just select which already-tested rows to show, no math runs in the browser or in this Flask app. 95% confidence band, widening with time, driven by recent volatility (same treatment for all three methods, so only the central assumption differs). Full-refresh every warehouse build -- always today's projections, not a record of past ones.
